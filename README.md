NovoSriti

Reconnecting the Past, Inspiring the Future

NovoSriti is an educational space exploration experience designed to help young learners (ages 9–14) discover the stories of abandoned and inactive NASA space equipment through interactive storytelling, missions, and scientific exploration.

Developed for **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**.

#Project Overview

Space exploration has left behind many fascinating pieces of equipment on the Moon, Mars, and beyond. Although some of these instruments are no longer active, their scientific contributions remain valuable.

NovoSriti transforms these forgotten space technologies into an engaging learning experience where students explore their history, understand their purposes, and discover what they taught humanity about space.

Our goal is to turn scientific knowledge into curiosity-driven exploration.

# Objectives

- Make NASA's space exploration history accessible to young learners.
- Explain abandoned and inactive space equipment through storytelling.
- Encourage critical thinking through interactive missions and quizzes.
- Connect real NASA scientific information with engaging gameplay.
- Inspire the next generation of space explorers.

#Key Features

1. Interactive Storytelling
A chapter-based storytelling experience introduces learners to the history and scientific importance of space equipment.

 2. Space Exploration Missions
Players complete seven sequential missions involving historical NASA equipment and missions:

1. Apollo Laser Retroreflector
2. Apollo Lunar Seismic Experiment
3. Viking Landers
4. Spirit and Opportunity Rovers
5. Curiosity Rover
6. Perseverance Rover
7. Ingenuity Mars Helicopter

 3. Interactive Gameplay
- Keyboard-based movement using WASD and arrow keys.
- Mission checkpoints.
- Interactive quizzes.
- Multiple-choice questions with explanations.
- Mission completion and progress tracking.

 4. Ask Engineer
An interactive assistant that provides page-specific answers and helps learners understand scientific concepts.

 5. Scientific Information Labels
To distinguish scientific facts from interpretation and creativity, the project uses three categories:

- FACT:Information supported by scientific sources.
- INFERRED: Reasonable interpretations based on available information.
- IMAGINED: Creative storytelling used to make learning engaging.

 6. Mission Completion Summary
After completing all seven missions, learners receive a performance-based achievement and can revisit the important information through a project summary.
